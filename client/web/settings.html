Screen settings

This screen shows the prayer times from your AthanBox and plays the azan through the TV.

You're changing a TV screen's settings from this phone or computer.

AthanBox

Type AthanBox's address, such as 192.168.1.20 or azan.local, or search the network for it. On AthanBox, Let TV screens connect on the TV app page must be on.

Azan volume on this screen:

Only this screen, for the azan and Makkah live. The phones, AthanBox's own screen and other TV screens keep theirs. On the prayer times, move the mouse for the volume buttons, or press + and − on a keyboard.

When Makkah live is on this screen. It goes quiet by itself while the azan plays.

Sound for each prayer

On this screen only: the phones and other screens keep the azan. Chime plays a short chime instead of the azan. Quiet plays nothing for that prayer, its reminder included.

Night sleep

The screen goes dark a while after Isha and comes back before Fajr. Anything AthanBox plays in the night wakes it, and so do a key and the mouse.

What sleeps
00:0006:0012:0018:0024:00

The computer switches off, and its own clock switches it back on before Fajr. Try it once: it switches off now for 3 minutes. If it doesn't come back by itself, switch on "Wake on RTC" or "Auto power on" in its BIOS, or let only the TV screen sleep.

Picture

Turn the picture

Turn it for a TV on its side. Keep it off the edges on a TV that cuts them off. The text size is this screen's own, on top of AthanBox's.
