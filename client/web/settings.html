Screen settings

This screen shows the prayer times from your Azan SIP Box and plays the azan through the TV.

Azan SIP Box

Type the box's address, such as 192.168.1.20 or azan.local, or search the network for it. On the box, Let TV screens connect on the TV app page must be on.

Azan volume on this screen:

Only this screen. The phones, the box's own screen and other TV screens keep theirs. On the prayer times, move the mouse for the volume buttons, or press + and − on a keyboard.
